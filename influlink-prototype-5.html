Terms and privacy

1. Using InfluLink

InfluLink connects creators and brands. You must be 18 or older and give accurate information when you create an account.

2. Escrow payments

Brands fund campaigns before work starts. Funds are held in escrow and released to the creator once the brand approves the deliverables, or automatically 7 days after submission if the brand does not respond.

3. Fees

InfluLink charges a 10% platform fee on each completed campaign. Card processing fees are shown before a brand pays.

4. Content and usage rights

Creators keep ownership of their content. Brands receive the usage rights listed in each campaign brief for the stated period.

5. Disputes

If a brand and creator disagree, either side can open a dispute. Our team reviews the brief, messages and deliverables and decides within 5 business days.

6. Your data

We only collect what we need to run the marketplace. You can download or delete your data from Settings at any time.

Common questions

When do I get paid?
Funds are released from escrow as soon as the brand approves your deliverables. Withdrawals reach your bank in 1–2 business days.
What is the platform fee?
InfluLink keeps 10% of each campaign budget. The amount you receive is shown on every campaign before you apply.
How do I get verified?
Connect at least one platform and add your verification code to your bio. Reviews usually take less than 24 hours.
What if a brand does not respond?
If a brand does not review your work within 7 days, the payment is released to you automatically.
How do I open a dispute?
Open the campaign, tap the menu and choose Open a dispute. Our team replies within 5 business days.
